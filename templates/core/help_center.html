Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload my resume?

Navigate to the Resume Analyzer page and click the upload area or drag and drop your file.

What file formats are supported?

We support PDF, DOCX, and TXT files up to 10MB in size.

How is my ATS score calculated?

Your resume is analyzed across 6 categories: contact info, skills, education, projects, experience, and links.

Can I practice interviews for any role?

We currently support Python, JavaScript, Data Science, DevOps, Full Stack, and General roles.
